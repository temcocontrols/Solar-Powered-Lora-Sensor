Temperature ┬À Humidity ┬À CO2 (opt.) ┬À TVOC (opt.) ┬À Ambient Light | Long-Range Wireless | Zero Maintenance | No Battery
The Temco Controls Indoor Ambient Solar-Powered Sensor Node is a completely
maintenance-free wireless monitoring solution for commercial and industrial buildings
where running cable is impractical or cost-prohibitive.
It collects temperature, humidity, and optionally CO2, TVOC, and ambient light data, then
transmits all readings wirelessly via long-range sub-GHz radio to the paired Temco
gateway ÔÇö which delivers the data to any BACnet or Modbus building automation system
over Ethernet or Wi-Fi.
The key innovation is the indoor-tuned solar cell. Standard outdoor solar panels are
designed for high-intensity sunlight. This sensor uses a specially engineered cell optimised
for the frequency spectrum of indoor LED and fluorescent lighting ÔÇö so it charges and
runs continuously under ordinary office, warehouse, or corridor light with no batteries and
no maintenance ever required.

  INDOOR SOLAR & SUPERCAPACITOR ENERGY STORAGE
Conventional solar panels target the 400ÔÇô700 nm visible spectrum at high outdoor irradiance (around 1,000 W/m┬▓). Indoor LED and fluorescent
lighting operates at far lower irradiance (100ÔÇô500 lux) with a different spectral profile. The solar cell in this sensor node is specifically engineered
and tuned for these indoor-light frequencies ÔÇö maximising energy harvest under typical building lighting conditions where a standard solar cell
would barely function.
Energy is stored in a supercapacitor or hybrid supercapacitor rather than a rechargeable lithium battery. This delivers major practical
advantages for building operators:
No battery replacement ÔÇö ever
Eliminates maintenance visits and associated labour costs
No hazardous materials
No lithium; compliant with shipping and building chemical policies
Building-friendly
Accepted in hospitals, data centres, and facilities with strict battery bans
Intelligent power management
CPU automatically slows reporting rate when stored energy is low, then resumes when light
returns
Long service life
Supercapacitors handle far more charge/discharge cycles than batteries

Avoid locations in permanent shadow unless a larger energy storage option is specified

Pair the sensor node to the gateway using the firmware pairing procedure (see gateway setup guide)
Confirm live values appear in T3000 before enabling trend log recording
